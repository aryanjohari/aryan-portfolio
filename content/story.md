## Notepad, my uncle's PC

*Where it started*

When I was a kid, my uncle was building a website in Notepad. He showed me how you type something here and the page changes there. I was already into sci-fi, space and technology, and seeing code turn into a page struck something in me. I wanted to do that.

## Koverify, then KRIL Digital

*Mumbai · 2021 – 2024*

That pushed me into computer science in Mumbai. During my degree I worked at Koverify, looking after the online shops of three wholesale clothing brands and building a catalogue app for one of them. I liked that I was learning how fashion wholesale works while I coded. After I graduated I joined KRIL Digital, a marketing agency, and built about 20 client websites. I wanted to learn social media, marketing and branding from the inside while I built the sites.

## Master's, GSTF, and SEO

*Auckland · 2025 – 2026*

Then I came to Auckland for a Master's at Unitec. I picked an AI thesis because AI is a fascinating space. It's us trying to recreate ourselves in a computer, and I wanted to understand the models and the maths in them. My thesis, [GSTF](/projects/gstf), tested whether a model could tell which tool made a deepfake video. It could on videos like the ones it trained on, but dropped to near chance on videos from a different source, and a bug I found in how it was measured became the paper I'm writing now. Alongside it I did part-time SEO for Specialist Support Services, because I wanted to get my hands dirty with a niche New Zealand business: how it works, how it talks to its customers, and what people are actually searching for.

## My own projects, then ADA v2 and Phosphene

*Now · 2026*

In my last months of study I started my own projects, to make something useful and prove I can finish things. That's [Sound Visualiser](/projects/sound-visualiser), music visuals that move with a song, [Prism](/projects/prism), a photo-styling web app, [PII Gateway](/projects/pii-gateway), an API that strips personal data before it's sent on, and [ADA](/projects/ada), an AI agent I built for my SEO work. All four are finished and tested.

These days I'm rebuilding [ADA](/projects/ada) from scratch and playing with [Phosphene](/projects/phosphene), which brings my [Prism](/projects/prism) and [Sound Visualiser](/projects/sound-visualiser) ideas together.
