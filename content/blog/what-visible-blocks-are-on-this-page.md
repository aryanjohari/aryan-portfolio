Our published pages include a visible title, a main heading, a clear direct answer, and content organized into sections. These sections can use lists or tables. Each page also includes images when needed, internal and external links, a canonical URL in the code, and visible published and modified dates. Depending on the page, we may also include a byline, an automation disclosure, and an optional call to action. We define these blocks to ensure our content is clear and useful to readers, and to meet web best practices.

### Visible Blocks on Our Shipped Pages

We consistently build our public pages with these key blocks. Some are required by our internal policy, while others align with recommendations from Google for good web content.

| Block             | What it is and why it's there                                                    | Source                 |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------- |
| **Title**         | The page's specific `<title>` text, concise and descriptive.                     | Google & Our Structure |
| **Main Heading**  | One distinct visible heading (e.g., `<h1>`) summarizing the page.                | Google & Our Structure |
| **Direct Answer** | The first sentence in the body, giving the reader the most trusted information.  | Our Structure          |
| **Sections**      | Organized content under headings, making the page easy to follow.                | Google & Our Structure |
| **Lists**         | Visible sequences within sections, used for ordered facts.                       | Our Structure          |
| **Tables**        | Visible comparisons within sections, used for comparing facts.                   | Our Structure          |
| **Images**        | Pictures with descriptive alt text, placed near the text they explain.           | Google & Our Structure |
| **Internal Link** | Crawlable links to other pages on our site, with clear anchor text.              | Google & Our Structure |
| **External Link** | Crawlable links to external sources, citing our facts.                           | Google & Our Structure |

### Our Commitment to Clear Content

We design our pages so that the most important information is always clear and accessible. The "Direct Answer" section ensures readers immediately find the core message. We structure content into sections, lists, and tables to improve readability, allowing readers to quickly scan or dive deep. Images are added only when they genuinely help explain the text, not just for decoration.

### Following Google's Guidance

Many of our structural choices are directly influenced by Google's best practices. Google emphasizes clear titles, descriptive headings, helpful images with alt text, and crawlable links. They also recognize the importance of canonical URLs for managing duplicate content signals and visible dates for content freshness. While Google does not mandate bylines or automation disclosures for indexing, they ask creators to consider them for helpful, reliable content. Our approach aligns with these recommendations to create useful, accessible web pages.

### What We Do Not Do

We do not implement arbitrary word counts, link counts, or image counts. Google confirms there are no ideal numbers for these. We do not use special schema types [for AI Overviews or AI Mode](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), as Google states none exist. We do not consider bylines, automation disclosures, visible dates, or images as strict requirements for a page to be published or indexed. Our pages avoid tactics like hidden text, keyword stuffing, or scaled content abuse. Each page offers unique value, ensuring the content itself is the primary focus.
