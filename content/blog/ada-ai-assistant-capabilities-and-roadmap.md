The ADA AI Assistant is already at work for operators. It can introduce itself and tell you what it does. It remembers your preferences and knows about named people you mention. ADA tracks your tasks, so you can ask what's due. It can also save files, like notes or data, to your computer and give you a receipt. A display strip shows your dues and saved files when you open the chat-home interface. You can also see a list of your saved items in an "artifact shelf." The system can provide brief check information about dues, and the groundwork is laid for future phone and Mac notifications.

Here's what's coming next for the ADA AI Assistant:

**Current Capabilities (Phase 0)**
These core functions are already available and form ADA's foundation:
*   **Self-introduction:** ADA can explain who it is and what it does from its core documents.
*   **Preferences:** It remembers your specific preferences.
*   **People and notes:** ADA keeps track of you and other named people, separate from a customer list.
*   **Task tracking:** It manages your tasks and shows you what is due.
*   **Local notifications:** Brief checks will show you what's due.
*   **File saving:** ADA can save Markdown or CSV files to your disk, giving you a receipt.
*   **Internal understanding:** It can answer questions about its own systems.
*   **Web to document:** ADA can fetch information from allowed websites and create summary documents.

**Next Steps (Phase 1)**
These features will enhance how ADA helps you stay informed and organized:
*   **Today view:** A dedicated strip in your main interface will show your dues, pending items, and saved files at a glance.
*   **Push notifications:** ADA will send pings to your phone or Mac to get your attention, but only after you agree to enable this feature.
*   **Improved task details:** ADA will get better at managing reminders, links to people, and links to saved files.
*   **Morning brief:** It will automatically prepare a summary for you each morning.
*   **Artifact shelf:** Your saved files will be easy to find and open outside of chat.
*   **Simple events:** ADA will track basic time-based commitments without needing a full calendar application.

**Future Plans (Phase 2)**
These are further enhancements planned for ADA:
*   **Inbox for files:** You will be able to drop files into a special inbox for ADA to process as part of your morning routine.
*   **Multi-day tasks:** ADA will manage complex, multi-day tasks that have multiple steps.
*   **Calendar synchronization:** It will connect with external calendar services like Google Calendar.
*   **Learned insights:** ADA will give you summaries of what it has learned about you.
*   **System management actions:** ADA will be able to perform actions related to its own internal systems, but only after you give explicit approval.

**Not Planned for Immediate Phases**
Some features are not scheduled for Phase 0 or 1. These include voice activation, integration with home assistants, wallet features, advanced sales funnels, full equality with advanced coding tools, support for 14 different communication channels, and new core memory systems.
